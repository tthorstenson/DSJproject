## **🧭 app-flow-and-roles.md — *User Flow and Roles***

### **App Flow**

**1\. Onboarding**

* Optional tutorial on Stoicism and reflection

* Anonymous journaling without signup

* Prompt first entry within 60 seconds

**2\. Home / Today View**

* Display date, quote, morning/evening prompt

* Input field for reflections

* Expandable commentary, mood input, AI tools

**3\. Timeline / Calendar**

* List or calendar of past entries

* Mood overview (optional)

* Tap to view/edit individual entries

**4\. Settings**

* Reminders (AM/PM)

* Mood tracking toggle

* Dark mode

* Export data (PDF/Markdown for premium)

* Account management

**5\. Premium Page**

* Benefits overview

* Purchase subscription or donate

* Unlock AI, exports, guided tracks

---

### **User Roles**

**Anonymous User**

* Journals locally with no account

* Offline and private

* Cannot sync, export, or use advanced tools

**Registered User**

* Sync entries with Firebase/Supabase

* Use across multiple devices

* Customize experience with settings

**Premium User**

* Export entries (PDF, Markdown)

* AI assistant for summaries/prompts

* Mood insights, streaks, guided journeys

* Access exclusive content and themes
